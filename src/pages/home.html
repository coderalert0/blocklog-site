What Blocklog does

Log a flight fast

Type the airports and your Out and In times, in UTC or the airports' local time, and Blocklog fills in block time, night (from the sun's position at each airport) and cross-country distance. Approaches by type and runway, holds, and "Save and add another" for the next leg. A half-entered flight is kept if the app closes.

Know you're current

Passenger currency by day and night (14 CFR 61.57(a) and (b)), instrument currency with the six-month grace period and IPC (61.57(c)), and your flight review (61.56), worked out from every flight you've logged, with the date each one runs out.

Totals for IACRA and 8710

Every column the 8710 form asks for, all time or for the last 7, 30, 90 or 365 days, plus your paper logbook's totals carried in as a starting point. Search or filter your flights and see the totals of just those.

Bring your logbook

LogTen's tab-separated export, ForeFlight's logbook CSV, MyFlightbook's CSV and most spreadsheets. You see every flight before anything is saved, rows that look wrong are listed, and a snapshot is taken first so an import can be undone.

Endorsements, signed and sealed

All 96 endorsements from FAA Advisory Circular 61-65K, filled in for your student and signed on screen. A signed endorsement is sealed: any later change shows as "changed after signing". Instructors can sign off a stack of lessons at once.

See where you've flown

Hours by month and by aircraft type, every route you've flown on a map, and the people you fly with: hours together, and dual given or received, instructor by instructor.

Photos on flights

A receipt, a paper logbook page or the view from the left seat, attached to the flight it belongs to.

Your data, never held hostage

Export everything as CSV for spreadsheets or as a full JSON backup (photos and signatures included) at any time, free. Blocklog keeps a snapshot every day for 30 days and before every import, and says plainly where your logbook is stored.

Help from a person

Help written for the device you're on, and an email button that reaches a person. You choose what's attached: app details by default, your logbook only if you switch it on.

Questions

Can I move my logbook from LogTen, ForeFlight or MyFlightbook?

Yes, free. Export from LogTen (Export Flights, tab-separated), ForeFlight (logbook CSV) or MyFlightbook (CSV), then open the file in Blocklog from Import & Export. You see every flight before it's saved, and an import can be undone from Backups.

Is Blocklog really free?

Yes: unlimited flights on one device, currency, totals, every import and export, endorsements and backups, with no ads and no time limit. Blocklog Pro ($49.99 a year, $4.99 a month or $149.99 once in the US) adds iCloud sync, the Mac app, printed logbook pages, more than one logbook and custom fields.

What happens to my logbook if I stop paying?

Nothing is taken away. Every flight stays on your device, you can keep adding flights, and you can export everything. Only sync, the Mac editing and printing stop.

Where is my logbook stored?

On your device and, if you turn on sync, in your own private iCloud database. There is no Blocklog server and no account; we never see your flights.

Which currency rules does Blocklog check?

The FAA's: passenger currency by day and night under 14 CFR 61.57(a) and (b), instrument currency under 61.57(c) including the six-month grace period and an IPC, and the flight review under 61.56. Check the rules yourself before you fly; the app is an aid, not a legal determination.

Can I print a logbook for Transport Canada or CASA?

Yes, with Pro. Neither regulator prescribes a page layout, only what each entry must hold (CARs 401.08 and CASR 61.345), so Blocklog's pages follow the column layout of each country's common paper logbook. CASA pages carry your date of birth and a true-copy certification on every page for your signature.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later, and Mac with macOS 14 or later (with Pro). One purchase covers every device on your Apple Account.
