Bringing your logbook in

How do I move from LogTen?

In LogTen, use Export Flights and choose tab-separated. Send the file to your iPhone, iPad or Mac (AirDrop, Mail or Files), then in Blocklog open Import & Export › Import a logbook file. Every flight is shown before anything is saved; rows that look wrong are listed with the reason.

How do I move from ForeFlight or MyFlightbook?

Export the logbook as CSV from either app or website, then import it the same way. Flights already in your logbook are skipped, so importing the same file twice doesn't duplicate anything.

I have a paper logbook. Do I have to type it all in?

No. Totals › Previous logbook takes your paper logbook's totals by category and class as a starting point, and every total and printout carries them forward. Currency counts only flights logged in Blocklog. To bring the flights themselves in, use Import & Export › Get a spreadsheet template, fill it in, and import it.

An import went wrong. Can I undo it?

Yes. A snapshot is taken before every import; restore it from Backups.

Logging and currency

Why is my night time or cross-country different from what I expected?

Night is worked out from the sun's position at each airport at the times you entered (the end of evening civil twilight to the start of morning civil twilight, 14 CFR 1.1), and cross-country from the airports' great-circle distance. Both are suggestions: change them in the flight if your records say otherwise.

Can I enter times in local time?

Yes. Settings › Type times in › Local uses each airport's own time zone; Blocklog stores UTC.

Which currency does the app check?

FAA passenger currency (day and night), instrument currency with the grace period and IPC, and the flight review, from every logbook together. In Currency you can also add your medical certificate (its class, exam date and your date of birth give the date it runs out for the privileges you use, 14 CFR 61.23(d)), and a student's signed solo endorsement shows the day its 90 days end. Check the rules yourself before you fly.

Can I track a currency the FAA rules don't cover, like NVG?

Yes. Currency › Add a currency: give it a name, pick what counts (flights, landings, approaches, any time column, or a field of your own) and how many in how many days. It's listed with the date it runs out, and included in reminders.

Can Blocklog remind me before my currency runs out?

Yes, if you switch on Settings › Remind me before currency runs out. A notification comes two weeks before each currency, your medical or a solo endorsement lapses. It's worked out on your device; nothing is sent anywhere.

Does it work with Siri?

Yes: "How many hours in Blocklog?", "Am I current in Blocklog?" and "Log a flight in Blocklog", in Siri and the Shortcuts app.

I deleted a flight by mistake. Can I get it back?

Yes. Tap Undo in the bar that appears after a delete: the flight and its photos come back, on every synced device. Older mistakes can be undone from a snapshot in Backups, and each saved flight lists its changes under History.

How do I see my hours in hours and minutes?

Settings › Hours › Hours and minutes (1:30). Totals, the logbook and reports all follow it; printed pages keep each regulator's format.

Sync and devices

How do I sync my iPhone, iPad and Mac?

With Blocklog Pro, open Sync and switch on Sync with iCloud on each device signed in to the same Apple Account. The fingerprint on the Sync screen matches on every device holding the same logbook.

I edited the same flight on two devices. Which one wins?

Both: changes merge field by field, so a time fixed on the iPad and a remark added on the iPhone are both kept. If the same field was changed on both, the later change wins.

Can I use the Mac app without Pro?

The Mac app is part of Pro. Without Pro it opens read-only, so a logbook already on your Mac stays readable and exportable after Pro ends.

Plans and refunds

What do I keep if I stop paying?

Everything: every flight, unlimited new flights on one device, currency, totals, imports, exports, endorsements and backups. Sync, editing on the Mac and printing stop.

How do I cancel or get a refund?

Cancel in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions, or from Blocklog Pro in the app. Refunds are Apple's: use Blocklog Pro › Request a refund, or reportaproblem.apple.com.

Does Lifetime Pro include future features?

Lifetime Pro includes everything in Pro for as long as Blocklog is offered. A future airline plan with roster import will be offered to Lifetime users for the difference.
