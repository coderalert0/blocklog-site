Privacy policy

Effective November 10, 2026.

Blocklog is built so your logbook stays yours. It has no account, no Blocklog server and no analytics, and we never see your flights.

What stays on your device

Your flights, aircraft, endorsements, signatures, photos, custom fields, settings and backups are stored on your iPhone, iPad or Mac (and in your device backups). Blocklog doesn't send them to us or to anyone else.

iCloud sync (Blocklog Pro)

If you turn on sync, your logbook is stored in your own private iCloud database (Apple's CloudKit) so your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account can read it. Only your devices can read that database; we can't. Apple's privacy policy covers iCloud. Turning sync off stops it; your devices keep their copies.

Photos, signatures and Face ID

Photos you attach to flights or endorsements are chosen with Apple's photo picker, which gives Blocklog only the photos you pick, and they're stored with your logbook. Signatures you draw are stored as images with the entry they sign. If you turn on the app lock, Face ID or Touch ID is checked by your device; Blocklog never receives biometric data.

Notifications

If you start a free trial, Blocklog schedules a reminder on your device two days before it turns into a paid plan. It's a local notification: nothing is sent to us.

Email to support

When you use Help › Email support, you send an email from your own mail app, and you see everything before it's sent. It includes a diagnostics file with the app's version, your device model and system version, your language setting, counts of your flights, aircraft, endorsements and photos, whether sync is on and when it last ran, recent error messages and two settings (local time entry and app lock). Your flights are attached only if you switch on "Include my logbook". We use what you send only to answer you, and delete it when we're done.

Sharing and exports

When you export a CSV, a backup, a PDF or an endorsement, you choose where the file goes (AirDrop, Mail, Files, a printer…). Blocklog only creates the file.

Purchases

Subscriptions and Lifetime Pro are sold and billed by Apple. Blocklog receives only whether you have Pro and when it renews, never your payment details.

Children

Blocklog isn't directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.
